About this project

This website was created as a university web technology assignment. It catalogues four flowering plant families found predominantly across tropical Asia and Africa: Myristicaceae, Chrysobalanaceae, Meliaceae, and Ebenaceae. For each family we describe two representative genera, and for each genus we profile two species in detail — covering their appearance, habitat, distribution, and human uses. All botanical information has been cross-checked against recognised references such as Kew's Plants of the World Online and the IUCN Red List.
