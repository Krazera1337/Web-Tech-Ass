Plant: Horsfieldia kingii (Hook.f.) Warb.

Family:
Myristicaceae

Genus:
Horsfieldia

Type:
Tree

Common Name: Ramtamul (Assamese)

Description: Horsfieldia kingii is a dioecious evergreen tree of the nutmeg family found across the eastern Himalayan foothills and mainland Southeast Asia. It was first described as Myristica kingii by Joseph Dalton Hooker in 1886, later transferred to Horsfieldia by Otto Warburg. Its large, bi-coloured arillate fruits are an important food source for hornbills and other large frugivorous birds, and its leaves are eaten by endangered capped langurs.

Plant: Parastemon urophyllus (Wall. ex A.DC.) A.DC.

Family:
Chrysobalanaceae

Genus:
Parastemon

Type:
Tree

Common Name: Nyilas Padang, Malas

Description: Parastemon urophyllus is a large evergreen tree of the cocoa-plum family, reaching up to 35-40 m tall with a long, straight, often buttressed bole. Its specific epithet 'urophyllus' means 'tail-leaf', referring to the tapering, pointed leaf tip. It is a common component of peat-swamp, kerangas (heath) and secondary forests across western Malesia.

Plant: Aglaia elliptica (C.DC.) Blume

Family:
Meliaceae

Genus:
Aglaia

Type:
Tree

Common Name: Lambunau (Malaysia)

Description: Aglaia elliptica is a tree in the mahogany family most often found along riverbanks and in forest subject to periodic flooding, from Indo-China through western and central Malesia. It bears orange, ellipsoid fruit with an edible, cranberry-like flavoured aril, and its bark and leaves have documented traditional medicinal uses in the Philippines.

Plant: Diospyros ebenum J.Koenig ex Retz.

Family:
Ebenaceae

Genus:
Diospyros

Type:
Tree

Common Name: Ceylon ebony, East Indian ebony, True ebony

Description: Diospyros ebenum is a slow-growing evergreen tree of southern India and Sri Lanka, historically the primary commercial source of the finest jet-black ebony timber. Its heartwood is dense, glossy black and famed for use in fine furniture, musical instruments and carved objects, which has led to overexploitation across much of its natural range.
